Courses Offered

Welcome to our courses page! We offer a wide range of programs designed to meet the diverse needs of our students.

Our courses are structured to provide both theoretical knowledge and practical skills, ensuring that our graduates are well-prepared for their future careers.

Explore our course offerings and find the program that best suits your interests and career aspirations. Our dedicated faculty and staff are here to support you every step of the way.

  • Introduction to Software Engineering: Core concepts of the software development lifecycle (SDLC), agile methodologies, and requirements analysis.
  • Object-Oriented Design & Analysis: Designing scalable software systems using design patterns, UML diagrams, and SOLID principles.
  • Software Testing & Quality Assurance: Unit testing, integration testing, automated QA frameworks, and continuous integration (CI/CD) pipelines.
  • Full-Stack Web Development: Building complete web applications covering both front-end presentation and back-end logic.
